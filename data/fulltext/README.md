# data/fulltext

Retrieved article texts live here on the researcher's machine and are **not redistributed**: they are
copyrighted news content. Each file carries a header (case, outlet, byline, recorded and page-confirmed dates,
headline, URL, retrieval timestamp) followed by the article body with site boilerplate removed.

To rebuild this folder, follow `docs/retrieval.md`: every item is listed by URL in `data/output/retrieval_log.csv`
and `data/output/search_candidates.csv`, so the corpus can be re-retrieved and verified against the logged word counts.
